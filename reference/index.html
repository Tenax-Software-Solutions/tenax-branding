Signal Orange — the accent rule

Orange is the identity's only accent and it is not a status colour. It marks brand and navigational emphasis: individual words, icons, active indicators, focus rings, small rules.

Do — use orange as text, icons and rules: threat activity increased 34% across monitored endpoints.
Don't — use orange as a large fill, or to mean "high severity". Severity is carried by filled badges; orange is never one of them.

Why hue alone can't separate them

Signal Orange sits at hue 10°. Every accessible red lands within ~12° of it, so brand orange and error red cannot be told apart by colour. They are separated by form instead: orange is never filled, status is always filled.

Inline orange next to filled status badges — different shapes, no ambiguity: the scan found 3 new hosts and flagged the following.

Adopting this in an app

  1. Copy assets/tokens/tenax.css and assets/fonts/ into your app.
  2. Link both; add class="tenax" to <body> for the base layer.
  3. Copy assets/favicon/ to your web root and link favicon.svg + favicon.ico.
  4. Pick one logo from assets/logos/ — *-inverse.svg on dark.
  5. Build with var(--tenax-*) only. Run tools/check-contrast.py if you add colours.
